<p>
The second stage of a period is an investment stage.  This this stage, you will decide how much you wish to invest in each stock 
out of your 100 units of cash.  
Enter your investment amounts in the boxes provided.  
As you enter your investment amounts, the available cash will decrease by that
 amount.  
You may invest any amount you wish up to your cash amount.
  You may not invest more than 100 units of cash.   
You are not required to invest all of your cash.
  You may invest nothing if you wish.  
</p>

<p>  </p>
<p>
You have as much time as you need to make your investments. 
Feel free to adjust them until you are satisfied. When you are satisfied with your 
investments, click on the “SUBMIT” button at the bottom of the screen.
</p>
