<p>Your task in each period is to predict the total value of each stock. This is equal to its stock-specific factor plus the index factor.
</p>
<p>For example, if the index factor were 10 and the stock-specific factors for A and B were &#8722;16 and &#8722;53, then the total values of Stocks A and B would be &#8722;6 (= &#8722;16 + 10) and &#8722;43 (= &#8722;53 + 10).
<br>(Note: you will not make a prediction about the index factor on its own, but it will contribute to each stock's total value.)
</p>
<p>However, you will not be given the exact factor values, and instead have to learn about them.
</p>
